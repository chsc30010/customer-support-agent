## When you want a person
Ask at any point and we will transfer you. On a call, say agent or press zero.
In chat or by text, just say so. You will not be asked to explain the problem
again, because the transcript goes with you.

## Support hours
Human agents are available 8am to 8pm Eastern, seven days a week. Outside those
hours a request is queued and answered first thing, and urgent security issues
are covered around the clock.

## Complaints
A formal complaint gets a named owner and a response within one business day.
Ask for it explicitly and it will be logged as a complaint rather than as a
support ticket, which changes how it is tracked and reviewed.
