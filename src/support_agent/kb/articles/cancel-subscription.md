## How to cancel
Open Account, then Subscription in the Kestrel app and choose Cancel plan. The
plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and
does not renew after that. Cancelling is immediate and does not need an agent.

## What you keep after cancelling
Cameras keep working after a cancellation. Live view, two way talk and motion
alerts are all free features. What you lose is cloud recording history beyond
24 hours, and recordings older than that are deleted 30 days after the plan
ends.

## Refunds on cancellation
Cancelling does not automatically refund the current period. If the plan renewed
within the last 30 days and you have not used cloud recordings since, we refund
that renewal in full on request.

## Pausing instead of cancelling
A plan can be paused for up to three months, which keeps existing recordings and
stops billing. This suits an empty house over a season better than cancelling
and resubscribing.
