## Updating your card
Go to Account, then Billing, then Payment method in the Kestrel app. The new
card is used from the next renewal onwards. If a payment has already failed, the
retry runs within an hour of saving the new card rather than waiting for the
next cycle.

## Why a payment was declined
Most declines come from the bank rather than from us, usually an expired card, a
billing address mismatch, or a block on recurring payments. The decline message
we receive rarely says which. Trying a different card is the fastest test.

## What happens after a failed payment
We retry a failed subscription payment three times over ten days. Cloud
recording keeps working during that period. If all three retries fail, the plan
drops to the free tier and existing recordings are kept for 30 days, so nothing
is lost if you fix payment within a month.
