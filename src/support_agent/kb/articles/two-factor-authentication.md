## Codes are not arriving
SMS codes can take up to two minutes. If none arrive, check the phone number
under Account, then Security, and confirm the country code is right. Switching
to an authenticator app removes the dependency on the mobile network entirely.

## Lost your second factor
Backup codes were issued when you turned two factor on, and any one of them
works in place of a code. Without a backup code, recovering an account needs
identity verification by a human agent, which takes about one business day.

## Turning two factor off
Two factor can be turned off under Account, then Security, but only after
signing in successfully. We cannot turn it off from our side, because doing so
on request would defeat its purpose.
