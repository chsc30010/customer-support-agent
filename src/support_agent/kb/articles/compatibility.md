## Phones and networks
The Kestrel app needs iOS 16 or Android 10 and later. Cameras need a 2.4GHz
wifi network. There is a web app at app.kestrelhome.com with live view and
recordings, though setup has to be done from a phone.

## Smart home platforms
Kestrel cameras work with Amazon Alexa and Google Home for live view on a smart
display. There is no HomeKit support. A local RTSP stream is available on the
Cam Pro only, and has to be turned on under camera Settings, then Advanced.

## Hardware differences
Cam 2 is battery powered with 1080p video and a 130 degree field of view. Cam
Pro is wired, records 2K, has a 160 degree field of view and colour night
vision. The Doorbell is wired to existing doorbell wiring and needs a 16 volt
transformer, which most homes already have.

## Weather and placement
All Kestrel cameras are rated IP65 and work outdoors from minus 20 to 50
degrees Celsius. They are not rated for submersion, so they should not be
mounted where water pools.
