## Not receiving motion alerts
Check three things in order: notifications are enabled for Kestrel in your phone
settings, the camera has motion alerts turned on under camera Settings, then
Motion, and the camera is not inside a scheduled quiet period. Quiet hours are
the most common cause and the easiest to miss.

## The app crashes or will not open
Update to the latest version first, then restart the phone. If it still crashes,
reinstall the app. Reinstalling does not affect your cameras or recordings, and
you will only need to sign in again.

## Live view is black or will not load
A black live view with a spinning indicator is a bandwidth problem rather than a
camera fault. Try the same camera over mobile data instead of wifi. If it works
on mobile data, the upload speed at the camera end is the limit.

## Recordings missing from the timeline
Recordings are kept for as long as your plan allows: 24 hours on the free tier,
30 days on Plus and 60 days on Family. Gaps shorter than that usually mean the
camera was offline at the time, which you can confirm in the event history for
that camera.
