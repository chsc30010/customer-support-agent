## Marked delivered but nothing arrived
Carriers sometimes scan a parcel as delivered several hours before it reaches
the door. Check with anyone else at the address and look in the usual safe
places first. If it still has not appeared 24 hours after the delivery scan, we
will replace the order. There is no need to file a claim with the carrier
yourself.

## Delayed shipments
Weather, customs and carrier backlogs are the usual causes. We will email you
whenever a promised delivery date moves. If an order is more than seven days
past its estimated delivery date you can choose a free replacement or a full
refund, whichever you prefer.

## Changing the delivery address
An address can be changed until the order dispatches, which is usually the same
or next business day. After dispatch the carrier controls the parcel and we
cannot redirect it, but most carriers let you redirect it yourself using the
tracking number.
