## Finding your tracking number
Every Kestrel order gets a tracking number as soon as the carrier scans the
parcel, which is usually within one business day of dispatch. You can find it in
the shipping confirmation email, or by opening the Kestrel app and going to
Account, then Orders. Orders are listed newest first, and the tracking number
sits under the order status.

## Tracking has not updated in days
A tracking number that has not moved usually means the parcel is in transit
between sorting facilities, where scans can be up to 72 hours apart. If there
has been no scan for four full business days, the parcel is treated as lost and
we will send a replacement at no cost. Contact us with the order number and we
will start that immediately.

## Typical delivery times
Standard shipping is three to five business days after dispatch. Express is one
to two. Orders placed after 2pm Eastern dispatch the following business day.
Cameras and doorbells ship from the same warehouse, so a mixed order arrives
together rather than in separate parcels.
