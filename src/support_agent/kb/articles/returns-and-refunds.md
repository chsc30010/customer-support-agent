## The return window
Any Kestrel device can be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full
refund. The device needs to be in working condition, but opened boxes and
missing packaging are fine. There is no restocking fee.

## Starting a return
Open the Kestrel app, go to Account, then Orders, and choose Return next to the
item. We email a prepaid shipping label straight away. Print it, attach it to
any box, and drop the parcel at a carrier location. If you cannot print, ask for
a QR code label instead and the carrier will print it for you.

## When the refund arrives
Refunds are issued the day the warehouse scans your return, and take three to
five business days to appear on a card or one to two on account credit. The
refund goes back to the original payment method. If that card has since been
closed, we can issue account credit or send a bank transfer instead.

## Faulty devices
A device that fails within the two year warranty is replaced, not refunded, and
you do not need to return the faulty one first. We ship the replacement as soon
as the fault is confirmed and include a label for the old unit.
