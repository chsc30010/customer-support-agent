## Charges you might not recognise
Kestrel charges appear as KESTREL HOME on a statement. A pending authorisation
of one dollar is a card check rather than a charge, and it disappears within
five business days.

## Charged twice for the same thing
A duplicate charge is almost always a failed payment that was retried. One of
the two will drop off within three business days on its own. If both have
settled, contact us with the date and amount and we refund the duplicate the
same day.

## Subscription renewal charges
Kestrel Cloud renews on the anniversary of the day you subscribed. We email a
reminder seven days before an annual renewal, but not before a monthly one. A
renewal charged in the last 30 days can be refunded in full if you have not used
cloud recordings since it renewed.

## Getting an invoice or receipt
Every payment has a receipt under Account, then Billing in the Kestrel app.
Invoices can be reissued with a company name and VAT number on them, which we
can do for any payment in the last two years.
