## Expected battery life
A Kestrel Cam 2 battery lasts about four months in a typical installation. Busy
locations facing a street or pavement can be closer to six weeks, because every
motion event costs battery.

## Battery draining unusually fast
The usual causes are motion sensitivity set high on a busy view, live view being
opened often, and cold weather, which can halve battery life below freezing.
Narrowing the motion zone to the area you actually care about is the single most
effective change.

## Charging
A full charge takes about six hours with the supplied cable. The camera keeps
recording while charging, so there is no gap in coverage. A battery that will
not hold charge at all is covered by the two year warranty.
