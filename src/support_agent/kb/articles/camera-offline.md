## First steps for an offline camera
Unplug the camera for ten seconds and plug it back in. Wait two full minutes for
it to rejoin the network. This clears the large majority of offline cameras.
Check that other devices in the house have working internet before going
further.

## Cameras that keep dropping off
Repeated dropouts are usually signal strength. In the Kestrel app, open the
camera, then Settings, then Network, and check the signal bars. Anything below
three bars will be unreliable. Moving the router or adding a mesh point fixes
this more reliably than anything we can change in software.

## Kestrel cameras need 2.4GHz wifi
Kestrel cameras join 2.4GHz networks only. Many routers give both bands the same
name, which usually works, but if setup fails repeatedly, split the bands into
two names in your router settings and join the 2.4GHz one.

## Factory reset
Hold the button on the back of the camera for 15 seconds until the light flashes
amber, then set the camera up again in the app. A factory reset does not delete
your recordings, which live in the cloud rather than on the camera.
