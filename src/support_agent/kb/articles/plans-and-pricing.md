## How much each plan costs
The free tier keeps 24 hours of recordings for one camera. Plus is five dollars
a month for 30 days of history on one camera. Family is twelve dollars a month
for 60 days of history across up to ten cameras. Annual billing on either paid
plan saves two months.

## What needs a paid plan
Live view, two way talk, motion alerts and the 24 hour timeline are free
forever. Longer recording history, person and package detection, and
downloading clips need Plus or Family.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the
end of the current period, and any recording history beyond the window of the
new plan is deleted 30 days later.
