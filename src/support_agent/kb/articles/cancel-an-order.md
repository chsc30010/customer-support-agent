## Cancelling before dispatch
An order can be cancelled at no cost any time before it dispatches. Open
Account, then Orders in the Kestrel app and choose Cancel order. The refund is
issued immediately and there is nothing to send back.

## Cancelling after dispatch
Once an order has dispatched it cannot be stopped, but you can refuse the
delivery or return it under the standard 45 day return window. Either way you
get a full refund, and refusing delivery is faster because the parcel turns
around at the depot.

## Changing items on an order
Orders cannot be edited after they are placed. Cancel the order and place a new
one, and if the price has changed in between we will honour the original price
if you contact us within seven days.
