## Resetting your password
If you cannot log in, the fastest fix is almost always a new password.
Choose Forgot password on the sign in screen and enter the email on the account.
The reset link arrives within a few minutes and is valid for one hour. If it has
expired, request a new one rather than reusing the old link. Check the spam
folder before assuming it did not arrive.

## Account locked after failed attempts
Five wrong passwords in a row locks an account for 30 minutes. The lock clears
by itself, and a successful password reset clears it immediately, so resetting
is faster than waiting.

## You no longer have access to the account email
If the email address on the account is one you cannot reach any more, we can
change it after verifying ownership another way. This always needs a human
agent, because it is the one change that could hand an account to the wrong
person.

## The app says no account exists
This nearly always means the account uses a different email, often a work
address or one with a typo. Try the other addresses you use before creating a
new account, since a new account will not see your existing cameras.
